The best business book I've read. It covers one of the hardest problems in making anything: going from zero to one. How do you get from not having something at all to having it?

One to a hundred is scaling a startup and everything that takes. Zero to one is making it in the first place: finding the idea and getting it off the ground. Peter Thiel explains it really well, and he's built some of the biggest companies in the game.

It taught me a lot about how startups run and what I should pay attention to.
