Percy Jackson is my all-time favorite book series. I started reading it as a little kid, and I've read it 30 to 40 times since. That's not an exaggeration, and it might be an undercount. I just reread the whole series front to back.

These books aren't about exploring heavy themes. They're just really well written and super fun. The characters are great: Percy is incredibly lovable, and so are the side characters. It's what got me into Greek and Roman mythology, and it's a big reason I love history now.

You're always rooting for Percy as he takes on something insane. No 12-year-old should be beating the god of war in a sword fight, fighting Kronos, or fighting his way out of literal hell, and he does all of it. It's a super fun action series.

Rick Riordan also does a really good job with important issues. His books have some of the best writing I've seen when it comes to diverse characters, in both race and sexuality, and they're a natural part of the story. Magnus Chase, a partner series, was the first time I read a book with a Muslim character in it, and I was really impressed by that.

This was the series I grew up on, and it's still my favorite.
