One of my favorite action books. The plot is incredibly gripping. It's set in a society where the gap between people is huge. The main character, Darrow, starts off as a slave in the colonies on Mars, already full of hatred for his "betters" after the love of his life is killed as a martyr. Then he finds out there's a whole world outside that he never knew existed.

The themes are great futuristic sci-fi: how humanity treats different groups of people, and how those groups react. The action is really cool too. It's all built around war games, and through them he learns how civilization is actually controlled and governed. The violent way he figures that out makes for a really gripping plotline.

It asks good questions. How do you punish people in a society that's supposed to stick together? How do you make moral laws in a world where the goal is beating the enemy? How do you trust anyone in a situation like that? The book explores all of them well.

The sci-fi side really speaks to me, especially the genetic changes, like people carved to have wings, and watching characters overcome those limits to lead people and become a political and military force.

The series is great up until the third or fourth book, where it takes a sharp turn downhill. But this first one was really fun to read, and I liked every character in it.
